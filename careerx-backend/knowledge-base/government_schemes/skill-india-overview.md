# Skill India Mission — Overview

Skill India is a Government of India initiative launched to train over 400 million people in India in different skills by 2022, and continues to operate as a broader skilling umbrella today.

## Key Components
- **PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana)**: Offers short-term skill training with monetary rewards on successful completion and assessment.
- **National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS)**: Provides financial support to employers for engaging apprentices.
- **Jan Shikshan Sansthan (JSS)**: Focuses on vocational skills for non-literate, semi-literate, and school dropouts.

## Relevance to Career Readiness
Students preparing for placements can use PMKVY-certified courses to supplement formal degrees, particularly in trades like IT-ITeS, retail, healthcare support, and electronics — useful for building a well-rounded skill profile alongside core technical skills.

## Maharashtra State Initiatives
Maharashtra State Skill Development Society (MSSDS) runs state-specific skilling programs aligned with the national mission, focusing on aligning training with local industry demand — directly relevant to SIH26134's problem statement on aligning skill development with industry requirements.
